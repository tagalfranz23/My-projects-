Privacy Notice

Barangay Minante 1 Integrated Management System

Information collected

The system collects the personal, residency, emergency-contact, account, and identity-document type information you provide for resident verification and delivery of authorized Barangay services.

If you are the head of the family, you may provide family members' names, dates of birth, and relationships. Family members are not given login accounts. Disability disclosure and disability details are optional; you may choose Prefer not to say. Family and individual disability details are restricted to authorized personnel. Population reports use aggregate counts and do not list these personal details.

How information is used

Authorized Barangay personnel use this information to review account eligibility, process service requests, communicate status updates, maintain audit records, and meet lawful administrative responsibilities.

Protection and access

Access is role-restricted. Sensitive profile and blotter information is not displayed publicly and is included in reports only when required for an authorized purpose.

Your consent

Registration records whether you agreed to this notice and the date and time of consent. Contact Barangay Minante 1 for questions or requests concerning your information.
